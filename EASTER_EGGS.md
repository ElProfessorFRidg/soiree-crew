# Easter eggs (JOY-07)

Hidden on purpose: none of these is mentioned in the app. Each one is harmless, needs no network and keeps working with Reduce Motion (the motion simply jumps to its end).

| Where | How | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Party screen, BAC at zero | Tap the big number ten times within a few seconds | A thank-you from the car, and a confirm vibration. Above zero the number ignores taps. |
| Recap, first slide | Long-press the party's name | The confetti falls again, with a heavy vibration. |
| Search | Type the word for cheers in any of the app's languages (tchin, cheers, salud, prost, santé, saúde, cin cin, за здоровье) | The search answers with a toast of its own. |
